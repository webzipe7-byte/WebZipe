The legal part

I don't want my website to get sued

This is what I would ask the AI.

I'm a startup lawyer, and every week I see founders who build their platform or website in one afternoon and forget the legal part. And it turns out that's exactly the part that costs them dearly later.

So, this is the prompt. Please:

  1. Add terms and conditions.

  2. Add a privacy policy.

  3. Add a cookie policy and check whether I need to ask for consent to use them.

  4. On every form, two separate checkboxes.

    One for terms and conditions, another for the privacy policy. No single tick for everything. Put a link next to the checkbox so the person can access both documents.

  5. Keep a record of when and how each person accepted.

    Make it traceable for when the SIC comes to ask me.

  6. Add a refund policy.

  7. Ask the user for, and use only, the data I truly need.

  8. Check that I have the rights to every image used on the page.

  9. Check the local laws that apply and make sure my page complies with them.

Watch out for number 5, the one almost everyone skips.

In Colombia, it's not enough that the person said yes: you have to be able to prove it.

If the SIC comes to you and you don't have that record, it's as if they had never authorized you.

What kinds of businesses can have a website?

Any kind: restaurants, shops, startups, independent professionals, service companies and much more. If you have a business, a website can help you show it off better.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site we design is fully responsive: it adapts to and works properly on phones, tablets and computers.

Can I add WhatsApp to my website?

Absolutely. In fact, it's one of the integrations we recommend most. It's the fastest, most direct way for customers to reach you.

Can I request design changes?

Yes. The design adapts to what you need: tell us your idea, your colors and your style, and we'll work on it with you until it looks the way you pictured it.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the complexity and scope of your project. Once we know the details of your business, we'll give you an estimated timeline on WhatsApp.

What information do you need to get started?

It helps to know what your business does, what products or services you offer and how you'd like customers to contact you. With that, we can start designing.

Can I customize the website for my business?

Yes, always. Every site is tailored to your business's identity, colors and content. We don't use fixed templates.

We also build online stores, digital catalogs, forms and booking systems, social media integration and ongoing maintenance. Tell us what your business needs and we'll recommend the best option.
